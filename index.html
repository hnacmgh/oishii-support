Oishii! Support

Support for Oishii! - Japanese Taste Words, an offline travel companion for understanding Japanese food words.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use the app offline?

Yes. The word and phrase content is stored in the app and can be used offline.

Where are my favorites stored?

Favorites are stored on your device and remain available offline. They do not sync through the app. Deleting the app also deletes its locally stored favorites.

How do I restore Premium?

Open the Premium screen and tap “Restore Purchases.” Use the same Apple Account that was used for the original purchase.

Does the app require an account?

No. Registration and login are not required.

Important Information

Oishii! is a language and travel aid for enjoying food in Japan. Meanings, pronunciations, and suggested phrases may vary depending on context, region, speaker, and restaurant.

Do not rely on this app to determine ingredients, allergens, dietary suitability, or food safety. If you have food allergies or dietary restrictions, always confirm directly with restaurant staff.

The app does not guarantee that a displayed or spoken message will be understood or fulfilled.
